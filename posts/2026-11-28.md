shafranov – a real‑time, free‑boundary tokamak plasma simulator for tcv, built in c++20.

i​t solves the grad‑shafranov equation on the actual coil and vessel geometry. the solver evolves coil, vessel and plasma currents, so vertical instability emerges naturally instead of being scripted.

the build uses cmake, ninja and opengl 3.3. a 0.1 ms timestep runs in ~0.2 s, letting you watch a discharge in slow motion. the vertical control loop can be toggled to see the plasma fall away.

i’m still polishing the feedback controller and adding more realistic wall models.

## Option 2: news take (linux kernel in wasm)

### X (125/280)

real linux kernel compiled to wasm? great for browsers, terrible for performance. guess we’ll finally run gcc in the sandbox.

### LinkedIn

unkernel: a real linux kernel compiled to webassembly.

the idea is neat – run a full kernel in the browser without any native code. could be a playground for teaching os concepts or a sandbox for experiments.

but the performance hit is massive and the syscall layer feels like a novelty hack. i’m not sold on it being production‑ready, but it’s a fun proof‑of‑concept.

will we see wasm‑based containers someday, or is this just a curiosity?
