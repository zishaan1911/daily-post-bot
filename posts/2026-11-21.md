## Option 2: news take (valkey drops skiplist for b+tree)

### X (118/280)

valkey ditching skiplist? finally trading 43% overhead for b+tree simplicity. guess sorted sets just wanted a spa day.

### LinkedIn

valkey just killed the skiplist, swapping it for a b+tree.

the skiplist was a clever compromise—fast inserts, decent reads, but a memory hog. the new b+tree shaves up to 43% overhead on sorted sets.

for us developers it means smaller footprints and potentially faster range queries. on the flip side, we lose the probabilistic balancing that made skips fun to tune.

anyone already feeling the performance lift, or still nostalgic for the old skiplist dance?
